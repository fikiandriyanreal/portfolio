About

I'm Fiki Andriyan, a web developer in Indonesia. I design and build websites from the first layout to the live page.

The work above covers tour operators in Bali and the Gili Islands, industrial and laboratory suppliers, an agro exporter, an aesthetic clinic with an online store and a premium car wash. Different industries, same job: explain what the business offers and make the next step obvious, whether that is a datasheet, a quote or a WhatsApp message.

Most sites are in English for visitors abroad, and some in Bahasa Indonesia for local buyers. I design the layout and build it in WordPress, including WooCommerce where a site needs a shop.
